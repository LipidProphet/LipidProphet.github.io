Quick answers

Can I use my existing search results?

LipidProphet accepts LDA and ALEX123 RDB tables and individual MS-DIAL peak-list text exports. If you use another engine, see how to prepare a compatible export.

How many replicate measurements do I need?

Use at least three analyses per scoring group. Groups are defined by sample type, acquisition group, and polarity. See how to set up the sample list.

What do I need for MS imaging?

Provide MS1 precursor and MS² fragment evidence, a supported search export, a matching spectral database, a sample list, and at least three analyses per scoring group. See the imaging requirements.

Where can I find a working example?

Select Download study dataset… in the desktop app. The quick start takes you from the study chooser to the scored output.
