From a spectral match to dataset-level confidence

A search engine proposes a lipid identity from a spectrum. LipidProphet checks how consistently its precursor and expected fragments appear across replicate measurements.

How the LP score works

Probability-based scoring

Four subscores combine precursor and fragment evidence. Even a weak, chain-specific fragment can contribute when it recurs across measurements.

False-positive control

LipidProphet checks for deterministic false positives when a competing lipid can explain a candidate’s fragments.

Across acquisition methods

Score low- or high-resolution LC–MS² and shotgun MSALL data. For MS imaging, check the input requirements.
