Scientific foundation

Confidence scoring built for real lipidomics complexity.

LipidProphet addresses limitations of per-spectrum similarity scoring by learning from precursor and fragment detection frequencies across an entire dataset.

The scoring idea

Dataset-level evidence

Cosine similarity and related scores evaluate spectral matches individually. In large studies, this can be challenged by diverse fragmentation mechanisms, low-abundance diagnostic ions, multiple levels of structural specificity, and multiplexed spectra from coeluting isomers or isobars.

LipidProphet instead computes confidence from statistical properties of the full dataset. Its four subscore functions capture intrinsic MS² features and work together with an algorithm that detects deterministic false-positive annotations.

Fragments that recur across a dataset have a lower probability of being noise. This makes the confidence measure less dependent on instrument-specific intensity patterns.
Validation scope

Tested across distinct analytical strategies.

LipidProphet was validated with murine liver and brain datasets spanning LC-MS² and shotgun MSALL, different fragmentation mechanisms, and low- and high-resolution detection.

LC

ALEX123 / Shotgun MSALL

Resolution range
